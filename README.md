# Breathing Rate Graph

A single-file graphing activity for Grade 8 STEAM, Body, Mind & Soul, Lesson 3: Circulatory and Respiratory Systems. It replaces a Jamboard extension activity that no longer works.

Students measure their own breathing rate (breaths per minute) at four activity levels: at rest, and during light, moderate, and intense exercise. The bar graph builds itself live as they type their measurements, on the same scale the answer key uses (0 to 40 breaths per minute, in steps of 2). Students title their graph, answer three questions about the pattern, then open the answer key to compare their pattern with the key's worked example. A summary compiles their title, data, and answers to print or copy into their portfolio.

Formative only: nothing is scored, nothing is submitted anywhere, and nothing leaves the student's browser. Single HTML file, no external resources, works offline.
